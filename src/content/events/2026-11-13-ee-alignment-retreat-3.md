## What it is about

We think technical work alone cannot align AI, in the same way that a constitution alone cannot
align a government with its citizens. So we start with ourselves and work outwards: to our
community, our environment and, in time, our technology.

We wrote more about the idea in
[What this retreat is about](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14Y-aC33-YyCNmp__GVyCLdMRUkrx59P31WtuS-DQfMA/edit?usp=sharing).

## When and where

The retreat runs for four days in Ooty, in the Nilgiris. It will be in one of these windows:

- 13-16 November 2026
- 20-23 November 2026
- 27-30 November 2026
- 4-7 December 2026

We will pick the window from the form responses.

## Themes

- **Cognitive gym.** Staying mentally sharp in a world where AI automates cognitive work.
- **Embodiment.** Walks in the forest, meditation and circling. Thinking with the whole body.
- **Sensemaking.** Mapping the state of the world, debates and predictions.
- **Relationships and community.** Building relationships, and staying in community after the
  retreat ends.

## How it runs

The retreat is an unconference: the people who come host most of the sessions. Bring your own
session. Past retreats had rationality workshops, poetry, prediction markets, a movie night, vibe
coding and bonfires. Some projects from those sessions still run.

We would also love sessions on:

- Visualisations and explainers that make a hard idea clear.
- Alignment between people, their tools and their surroundings.
- Education in a world changed by AI.
- The metacrisis: the patterns behind the crises we face.

## Why we do this

We value local initiatives, and a community with room for different views on AI alignment. We
want a place to make sense of AI and technology together, without deferring to whoever sounds
most certain. Most of all, we hope for rich friendships and the work that grows out of them.

## Questions

### Who can come?

Bring a question about AI that you cannot answer alone. A job in AI is optional.

### What happens at the retreat?

The participants host most of the sessions. At Retreat 2.0 they ran debates, a tabletop
exercise, a workshop to build small apps and a poker night. Retreat 3.0 adds a cognitive gym,
where you build things by hand. It has a robot arm and a computer that runs on marbles.

### Must I run a session?

Yes. Each participant brings one session. A session can be a talk, a game, a puzzle or an open
question. We accept sessions that are half finished.

### How large is the group?

Retreat 2.0 had 15 participants, enough for one circle around a campfire. We plan a small group
again, so we cannot give a place to everyone who fills the form.

### How fit must I be?

Fit enough for a walk in the forest. We walk at the pace of the slowest walker and stop for
good views.

### Can I come for part of the retreat?

No. Each participant stays for all four days, and work stays at home.

### How much does the retreat cost?

The cost is about Rs 16,000 for the four days. We have limited funding if the cost is what
stops you.

### Does the form commit me to come?

No. The form tells us that you are interested and which dates work for you. It takes 5 to 15
minutes, which depends on how much you like to write. Fill it by 10 October.

### How do I reach Ooty?

Travel to Coimbatore by air or by train. From Coimbatore, the road to Ooty takes about three
hours. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway runs from Mettupalayam to Ooty. It is slow, and that is the
reason to take it.
