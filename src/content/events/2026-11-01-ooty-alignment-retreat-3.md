We are back with a new iteration of the Ooty Alignment Retreat. The expression of interest
form takes 5 to 15 minutes. Please fill it by **10 October**.

We plan to run more iterations on other dates and in other places. Fill the form even if South
India or November does not work for you.

## When and where

Ooty, in the Nilgiris, in November 2026: four nights over a weekend. We will set the exact
dates from the form responses.

## This time

- **Cognitive gym.** Staying mentally sharp and healthy in a world where AI automates
  cognitive work.
- **Embodiment.** A full day of trekking. Thinking with the whole body, and room to be creative.
- **Relationships and community.** Building friendships, and staying in community after the
  retreat ends.

## How it runs

The people who come build the sessions together. Everyone brings something to run: a talk, a
workshop, a game, a meditation, a question. We would also love sessions on:

- Visualisations and explainers that make a hard idea clear.
- Forecasting and sensemaking: falsifiable predictions, and changing your mind as reality answers.
- Alignment between people, their tools and their surroundings.
- Education in a world changed by AI.
- The metacrisis: the patterns behind the crises we face.

## Why we do this

We value local initiatives, and a community with room for different views on AI alignment. We
want a place to make sense of AI and technology together, without deferring to whoever sounds
most certain. Most of all, we hope for rich friendships and the work that grows out of them.

## Questions

### I don't work in AI. Is this for me?

Yes. Bring curiosity and a question you can't put down. You won't need a reading list or the
right vocabulary.

### What do we actually do all day?

The people who come run the sessions. Last time that meant debates, a workshop where people
built small apps, a tabletop crisis game, poker and a movie night. This time we add a full
day's trek and a cognitive gym of puzzles and things to build by hand.

### Do I have to run a session?

Yes, and it can be small and rough. Bring a puzzle you love, or a half-baked idea you want
people to poke at.

### I won't know anyone.

Most people don't on day one. It's a small group, fifteen of us last time, so that lasts about
one dinner.

### How fit do I need to be for the trek?

Fit enough to walk for a day with breaks. We go at the group's pace.

### Can I come for a day or two, or work from there?

Please come for all of it and leave work at home. The retreat depends on everyone being fully
there.

### What does it cost?

About Rs 16,000 for the four nights. We'll confirm it when we share the dates.

### Is filling the form a commitment?

No. It tells us you're interested and which dates suit you, and it takes 5 to 15 minutes. We
keep the group small, so we may not have room for everyone.

### How do I get to Ooty?

Reach Coimbatore by flight or train, then it's about three hours up the hill by road. The toy
train from Mettupalayam is the slow, scenic way, if you can get a ticket.

### What was the last one like?

Read [Reflections from Ooty Retreat 2.0](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/KerjdwMehqrHDHEhJ/reflections-from-ooty-retreat-2-0).
It covers what worked and what went wrong.
