We are back with a new iteration of the Ooty Alignment Retreat. The expression of interest
form takes 5 to 15 minutes. Please fill it by **10 October**.

We plan to run more iterations on other dates and in other places. Fill the form even if South
India or November does not work for you.

## When and where

Ooty, in the Nilgiris, in November 2026: four nights over a weekend. We will set the exact
dates from the form responses.

## This time

- **Cognitive gym.** Staying mentally sharp and healthy in a world where AI automates
  cognitive work.
- **Embodiment.** A full day of trekking. Thinking with the whole body, and room to be creative.
- **Relationships and community.** Building friendships, and staying in community after the
  retreat ends.

## How it runs

The people who come build the sessions together. Everyone brings something to run: a talk, a
workshop, a game, a meditation, a question. We would also love sessions on:

- Visualisations and explainers that make a hard idea clear.
- Forecasting and sensemaking: falsifiable predictions, and changing your mind as reality answers.
- Alignment between people, their tools and their surroundings.
- Education in a world changed by AI.
- The metacrisis: the patterns behind the crises we face.

## Why we do this

We value local initiatives, and a community with room for different views on AI alignment. We
want a place to make sense of AI and technology together, without deferring to whoever sounds
most certain. Most of all, we hope for rich friendships and the work that grows out of them.
