We are back with a new iteration of the Ooty Alignment Retreat. The expression of interest
form takes 5 to 15 minutes. Please fill it by **10 October**.

We plan to run more iterations on other dates and in other places. Fill the form even if South
India or November does not work for you.

## When and where

Ooty, in the Nilgiris, in November 2026: four nights over a weekend. We will set the exact
dates from the form responses.

## This time

- **Cognitive gym.** Staying mentally sharp and healthy in a world where AI automates
  cognitive work.
- **Embodiment.** A full day of trekking. Thinking with the whole body, and room to be creative.
- **Relationships and community.** Building friendships, and staying in community after the
  retreat ends.

## How it runs

The people who come build the sessions together. Everyone brings something to run: a talk, a
workshop, a game, a meditation, a question. We would also love sessions on:

- Visualisations and explainers that make a hard idea clear.
- Forecasting and sensemaking: falsifiable predictions, and changing your mind as reality answers.
- Alignment between people, their tools and their surroundings.
- Education in a world changed by AI.
- The metacrisis: the patterns behind the crises we face.

## Why we do this

We value local initiatives, and a community with room for different views on AI alignment. We
want a place to make sense of AI and technology together, without deferring to whoever sounds
most certain. Most of all, we hope for rich friendships and the work that grows out of them.

## Questions

### Who can come?

You do not need to work in AI. The retreat is for anyone who wants to ask questions about AI
and how to prepare for it.

### What happens at the retreat?

The participants run the sessions. At Retreat 2.0 they ran debates, a tabletop exercise, a
workshop to build small apps and a poker night. Retreat 3.0 adds a cognitive gym and a trek
that takes a full day. In the cognitive gym you solve puzzles and build things by hand.

### Must I run a session?

Yes. Each participant brings one session. A session can be a talk, a game, a puzzle or an open
question.

### How large is the group?

Retreat 2.0 had 15 participants. We plan a small group again, so we cannot give a place to
everyone who fills the form.

### How hard is the trek?

The trek takes a full day. We walk at the pace of the group and stop for breaks.

### Can I come for part of the retreat?

No. Each participant stays for all four nights and does no other work during the retreat.

### How much does the retreat cost?

The cost is about Rs 16,000 for each participant, or Rs 4,000 for each night. We will confirm
the cost when we set the dates.

### Does the form commit me to come?

No. The form tells us that you are interested and which dates work for you. It takes 5 to 15
minutes. Fill it by 10 October.

### How do I reach Ooty?

Travel to Coimbatore by air or by train. From Coimbatore, the road to Ooty takes about three
hours. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway runs from Mettupalayam to Ooty.
